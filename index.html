The thinking behind the work ↓
( Design with a point of view )

I’m Manu, a brand designer based in Zaragoza. I connect visual identity, marketing thinking and creative AI to shape how brands look, communicate and are perceived. My background in graphic design, animation and 3D brings a visual storytelling perspective to every project.

Ch. One

I Strategy

A distinctive brand starts with a clear position. I look at its audience, context and ambition to define what it stands for, what makes it relevant and how that should come through visually.

That thinking guides the creative work: a recognisable identity, a focused message and applications that support the same idea across different channels.

Ch. Two

II Art direction

Typography, imagery and composition shape the way a brand is perceived. I use them to create a visual language with character, from the first campaign image to the smallest application.

My illustration and editorial work sharpen that attention to detail. I develop concepts through visual research and experimentation, then refine the relationships between image, message and format.

Ch. Three

III Creative AI

I use generative AI for visual research, concept development and image exploration. It helps me test directions quickly, then refine the strongest ideas through design, composition and art direction.

My animation and 3D background helps me think beyond a single image. I explore how a visual idea can move, respond and become part of a broader brand experience.

Ch. Four

IV Brand experience

I’m especially interested in fashion, lifestyle and luxury, where perception is shaped by every detail: the identity, the imagery, the packaging and the experience.

I look for consistency across those encounters. The aim is to carry the same point of view from a campaign to a screen or a physical space, with a clear and recognisable character.
